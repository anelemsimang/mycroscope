# Organization Sign-Up Guide

## How to Register Your Organization

### Step 1: Get Admin Approval
1. Contact the system administrator to create your organization
2. Admin will create your organization in the admin dashboard
3. Admin will provide you with:
   - **Organization Name**
   - **Secret Key**
   - **Approval Status** (should be "active")

### Step 2: Sign Up on Mobile App
1. Open the **Mycroscope Mobile App**
2. On the login screen, tap **"Register Organization"**
3. Enter your organization details:
   - **Organization Name**: (exactly as provided by admin)
   - **Secret Key**: (exactly as provided by admin)
4. Tap **"Verify Organization"**
5. Once verified, complete your account details:
   - **Full Name**: Your name as organization owner
   - **Email**: Your email address
   - **Password**: Create a secure password
   - **Contact Person**: Primary contact (optional)
   - **Phone**: Contact number (optional)
6. Tap **"Create Account"**

### Step 3: Access Your Dashboard
1. After successful registration, you'll be automatically signed in
2. You can now access your organization dashboard
3. Use your email and password for future logins

### Step 4: Manage Your Organization
- **Register Employees**: Add employees to your organization
- **View Analytics**: Monitor productivity and activity
- **Generate Reports**: Create detailed reports
- **Manage Settings**: Configure organization preferences

## Security Features
- ✅ **Secret Key Validation**: Only approved organizations can register
- ✅ **Admin Verification**: Organizations must be pre-approved
- ✅ **Secure Authentication**: Email/password login system
- ✅ **Organization Isolation**: Each organization's data is separate

## Troubleshooting
- **"Invalid organization name or secret key"**: 
  - Double-check the credentials from admin
  - Ensure organization is approved (status: "active")
- **"Organization not found"**: 
  - Contact admin to verify organization exists
  - Check if organization name matches exactly
- **"Organization not active"**: 
  - Contact admin to activate your organization

## Example Flow
1. **Admin creates**: "Acme Corp" with secret key "acme_2024_key_123"
2. **You sign up**: Enter "Acme Corp" and "acme_2024_key_123"
3. **System verifies**: Matches credentials and approves registration
4. **You create account**: Set up your owner credentials
5. **Access granted**: Full access to organization dashboard
