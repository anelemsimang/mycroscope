A productivity tracker for software agencies

18 October 2026

A software agency sells hours and outcomes. The task board knows the job. It does not know that the afternoon was the editor, the browser, or a call. A long-tail search for a tracker built for development teams is usually that gap.

What you can read

  • Time in editors, browsers, terminals and the other apps on the machine. Mycroscope does not plug into an IDE and does not read the code.
  • A daily goal for productive time, on Workforce Management and above, and workload against that goal.
  • Teams in the reports, so a studio can separate one client group from another if you have set the groups up that way.
  • Remote and office days, when a location rule is set.

What it will not do

It will not replace the board where tasks live. It will not score a developer by lines of code. It will not tell you the ticket was the right ticket. Output stays in the tools that already track output.

Free covers up to 3 people, which is enough for a small studio to read one week before buying seats. Paid plans are in rand, billed yearly.
