Billing

How the yearly price is worked out

8 October 2026

The prices on the plan cards are per person, per month, and they are before VAT. The payment itself is for a whole year. There is no monthly debit.

The sum

Take the monthly price, multiply by 12, then multiply by the number of active people. With VAT at 15%, that total is what the card is charged.

  • Work Activity Tracking is R100 a month. One person for a year is R1,200 before VAT, or R1,380 with VAT.
  • Workforce Management is R200 a month. One person for a year is R2,400 before VAT, or R2,760 with VAT.
  • Productivity Optimization is R300 a month. One person for a year is R3,600 before VAT, or R4,140 with VAT.

Free stays at R0 and is limited to 3 people.

Changes during the year

Moving up from Free charges a full year, and the paid period starts when the payment is confirmed. Moving up from one paid plan to another during the year charges the price difference for the days still left, and the end date stays the same. Moving down, or cancelling, waits until that end date. The unused months are not refunded.

If a renewal is missed, the paid plan remains for 7 days. After that the account moves to Free. People above the Free limit are paused. They are not deleted.
