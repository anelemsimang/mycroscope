When the plan changes

  • At checkout you choose how many seats to buy. Seats bought together share one end date. The charge includes VAT.
  • A seat added later is charged for its own year, with VAT, and ends on its own date.
  • Removing a seat waits until that seat's end date. The unused time is not refunded, and the seat can be used until then.
  • Moving up from a paid plan can start today or at the end of the current term. Starting today charges one year of the new plan for the seats already purchased, minus the unused part of the current plan on each seat. Every seat then ends one year from today. Waiting charges nothing today. The new plan starts on the last seat's end date only after a year of the new plan is paid. If that payment is not made, the change does not start.
  • Moving from Free to a paid plan charges a full year for the seats you choose.
  • A downgrade or cancellation starts when the last seat's year ends. There is no refund for the unused months.
  • If a seat is not renewed, it ends on its date. After the last seat's grace of 7 days the account moves to Free. Extra people are paused. They are not deleted.
