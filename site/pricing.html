When the plan changes

  • An upgrade starts when the card payment is confirmed. Moving up during the year charges the price difference for the days left in that year.
  • Moving from Free to a paid plan charges a full year.
  • A downgrade or cancellation starts at the end of the paid year. There is no refund for the unused months.
  • If a renewal is missed, the paid plan stays for 7 days and then the account moves to Free. Extra people are paused. They are not deleted.
