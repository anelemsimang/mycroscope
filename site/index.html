Questions the record can answer

The figures come from the desktop app on each computer, not from a guess about how busy a team feels.

Who is within the working hours?

Schedule adherence compares activity with the company, team or personal hours you set.

Where did the time go?

App and website totals show which tools took the most active time in the period you choose.

Who is under or over a goal?

Workload compares productive time with each person’s daily goal. Capacity turns a team gap into hours.

Are people in the office?

On the Workforce plan, office-day reports compare declared work location with the rule you set.

Which AI tools are in use?

The same plan can list known AI tools, who used them, and whether that use is approved.

What should a manager read?

Productivity Optimization writes a report from the stored figures and can include the same charts in a PDF.

One product. Four plans.

Each paid plan adds to the one below it. Prices are per seat, per month, and you choose how many seats to buy for the year.

How a company starts

1. Create the account

The owner registers the organisation, chooses a plan, and invites people with an activation code. The desktop app also asks for the company install key.

2. Publish the notice

Employees acknowledge the monitoring notice before recording follows the company’s rules. The notice states what is collected and how long it is kept.

3. Read the record

Managers use the dashboard, insights and exports. Employees can open their own activity. Operators who support the platform cannot read customer activity.

Common questions

Does Mycroscope photograph the screen?

No. It records activity such as the app, the website, and active or idle time, according to the settings the company publishes in the notice.

How is a paid plan charged?

The monthly price is before VAT. At checkout you choose how many seats to buy. The card payment is that price times 12, times those seats, plus 15% VAT when VAT is on. A seat added later has its own year and its own end date.

What happens on the Free plan?

Up to 3 people and 30 days of activity. Live status, teams, goals and the paid reports stay hidden rather than showing empty placeholders.
