For teams everywhere

A daily productivity tracker online

Mycroscope is productivity tracker software for teams in any country. The Windows app records the computer day. The website shows that day as a visual productivity dashboard.

Automated time tracking SaaS

People do not fill in a timesheet. After they acknowledge the monitoring notice, the desktop app records active, idle and away time, plus the application and the website. Managers open the same record online. Prices are in rand, and an organisation anywhere can open an account.

Employee focus tracker

Work is classified as productive, unproductive or neutral, including rules the organisation writes. A daily goal compares productive time with the hours you set. Green is on the goal, amber is under it, and red is over it. That is the focus record: where the computer day went, not a guess about how busy someone felt.

Visual productivity dashboard

The dashboard graphs productive hours, each person’s share of a goal, the apps that took the most time, and productive hours by team. The same charts can go into a PDF. How to read the charts.

Productivity software for teams

Free covers up to 3 people. Paid plans add live status, schedules, website and app totals, alerts, team goals, workload and written reports. A feature that is not in the plan is hidden. The feature list and the prices.

Productivity tracker for remote teams

The dashboard is online, so a manager can read the day from anywhere the person works. On Workforce Management, work-location reports compare office and remote days with the rule you set.

Productivity apps for developers

Developers spend the day in editors, browsers, terminals and design tools. Mycroscope totals that application time. It does not install inside an IDE and it does not read the code.

Freelancer productivity tool

A freelancer can be the only person on the account and can open their own activity. Free is enough for up to 3 people. The same notice and acknowledgement apply before recording starts.

Agency time, beside your task list

An agency can see where a team’s hours went, by person, app and goal. Mycroscope is the productivity record next to task management the agency already uses. It does not assign tasks, and it is not a task board.
